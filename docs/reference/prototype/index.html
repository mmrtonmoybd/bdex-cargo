01 — ABOUT BD-EX CARGO

A cargo service built for the Bangladeshi community abroad

BD-EX Cargo is an international courier delivery aggregator and broker. After a decade of express shipping in the United Kingdom, we began serving Australia and North America in April 2012 — shipping goods to loved ones in Bangladesh with maximum convenience and guaranteed satisfaction.

Our network runs through drop-off stations in Sydney and in New York and New Jersey, backed by daily consolidated shipments, customs clearance handled end to end, and free home delivery across Bangladesh.
